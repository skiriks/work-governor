# Component origins

Work Governor is a personal project by Kyrylo, developed with AI assistance.

The bundled hierarchy icon was reconstructed with AI assistance from the author's supplied plugin screenshot. It is a visual identifier and does not imply OpenAI endorsement.

It includes adapted material from [Matt Pocock's skills repository](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills), licensed under MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Matt Pocock. The full upstream notice is preserved in [LICENSES/MIT-mattpocock.txt](LICENSES/MIT-mattpocock.txt).

The inherited material was revised, condensed, and integrated into this plugin. Relevant source methods include `research`, `writing-for-agents`, `prototype`, `to-questionnaire`, `grilling`, `domain-modeling`, and `grill-with-docs`. Their descendants appear in the research and agent-writing skills, Governor's clarification workflow, and internal prototype, questionnaire, domain, and continuity guidance. Historical development records establish that lineage; not every current sentence can be mapped to a particular upstream revision.

Work Governor's integration, Codex-specific invocation and host-boundary instructions, task coordination, and release documentation were developed for this project. This is not an official Matt Pocock or OpenAI plugin, and no endorsement is implied.

The project license covers original contributions; the upstream MIT notice remains applicable to inherited material. No third-party skill must be separately installed to use the four bundled skills. Other skills mentioned as optional capabilities are not distributed here.
